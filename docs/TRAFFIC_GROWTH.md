# Audience growth tools

Open **Traffic & SEO ? Reach more readers**.

## Promote a published article

1. Choose a confirmed published article and click **Prepare promotion pack**.
2. Copy the LinkedIn, developer-community or newsletter text. Review the saved excerpt and adapt it to your audience.
3. Share the corresponding tracked link where relevant and permitted. The community version discloses that the article is from your blog. Studio does not submit messages to external accounts.
4. Keep each channel's original link so GA4 can attribute sessions. Campaign names use `article-<Studio article ID>` and remain stable across visits.

Packs are created on demand from saved content without an AI API call. Scheduled/draft posts are excluded. Generating a pack never changes the article or publishing approval.

## Measure readers and international reach

Click **Read audience and search queries** after choosing 7, 28 or 90 completed days. Studio reads top GA4 country, source/medium, campaign and page breakdowns, filtered to the selected blog hostname. Each includes sessions, engaged sessions, engagement rate and pageviews. This requires the existing GA4 property and read permission in Blog connections; no new key is needed.

Sharing campaigns identify attributed measured sessions, not raw link clicks. Analytics collection, consent, blockers and processing affect coverage. Empty rows remain unknown; separate breakdowns are not added into one visitor count. The selected property timezone is used when configured; otherwise UTC is shown explicitly. Google reads are reused for five minutes and report failures are isolated per breakdown.

## Plan useful improvements

Search Console query/page rows suggest existing answers to improve. **Save research brief** adds a private PLANNED item to Topic planner; repeating the action reopens the same brief. Add primary sources and check the existing answer before drafting. This does not start generation or publication. Query results are limited top rows and do not guarantee traffic, ranking or current indexing.

## Connect related posts

The pack lists relevant published posts on the selected blog. Copy a public link, or open an editable draft's **Related published posts ? Find related posts** section to insert a suggestion before saving. Existing published bodies remain unchanged. The generation workflow already receives existing internal-link candidates.

## Operational boundaries

These tools do not buy traffic, manufacture visits, click ads, request indexing or publish externally. Daily Blogger automation and the owner policy are unchanged. Promote real useful content and compare actual results over time; earnings are not guaranteed.
